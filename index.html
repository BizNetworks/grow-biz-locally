Running an SEO department is not why you started an agency

Local SEO is simple to sell and tough to deliver. Most owners try one of four routes first.

×

Hire a specialist

A $60,000 to $90,000 salary for one person who can juggle 8 to 10 clients before quality drops. Three signed clients barely cover it, and you carry the risk if they leave.

×

Do it yourself

Each hour inside a Business Profile is an hour you are not selling. The work stalls and the pipeline thins.

×

Use a generalist

A do-everything shop treats a neighbourhood plumber like a national store. The client gets vague content and stops picking up your calls.

×

Let it slide

Nothing visibly breaks for ninety days. Then the client asks why they are still missing from map results.

FAQ

Questions agencies ask before starting

About the arrangement

What does white label mean here?

Everything carries your logo and brand: reports, audits, content, emails and calls. We never appear in what your client sees, we will sign your NDA, and we never contact your client.

Could my client find out about you?

Not from us. We name no partners publicly and show no partner logos. Nothing we publish identifies a client.

Can your team join client calls?

Yes. We join as your team and introduce ourselves as your local SEO lead, not as Grow Biz Locally.

How big does my agency need to be?

There is no minimum. Solo consultants add a service line without hiring, and larger agencies use us for overflow, from one client to fifty.

Getting started

How quickly can we begin?

Access is verified within a business day, the audit comes back before work starts, and delivery begins that same week.

What do you need from me?

Profile manager access, website admin, Search Console and Analytics if it exists, plus a short client brief on services, service area and anything they will not say publicly.

What if the client's website is weak?

The audit tells you exactly what is wrong and we work within it. If the site is the real bottleneck, we say so.

Is there a trial?

No free trial, but no contract either. Send one client, review the month-one audit and report, then decide. Your exposure is $500.

The work itself

When will map results move?

Most locations show meaningful movement in 3 to 6 months. New listings, suspended profiles and dense metro areas take longer, and you get a specific estimate after the audit.

What if a profile gets suspended?

We handle reinstatement at no extra cost, including the appeal, documentation and follow-up.

Is the content written in-house?

Yes. Writers are briefed on the trade before they write a word.

Do you handle multi-location clients?

Yes. $500 for the first location and $250 for each extra location on the same client, each with its own grid, citations and content.

What about businesses without a storefront?

Fully supported, and common in home services. They need a different approach to service pages and citations, and we run it.

Money and terms

What should I charge my clients?

Most partners charge $1,500 to $3,000 a month for a single location, a 67% to 83% margin. The price is your call, and we can share what partners in your vertical charge.

Is the bundle a six-month contract?

No. It is prepaid for six months at a discount, with no contract, notice period or cancellation fee on anything we sell.

What happens if I cancel?

Every login, asset, document, report and content file is handed over within 5 business days, and we never approach your client afterwards.
